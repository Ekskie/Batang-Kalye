ABOUT THIS PACK
Batang Kalye Game Audio Collection — Vol. 01 is a curated collection of stylized sound effects and voice lines developed for video games and interactive media. 
Designed to complement the personality and atmosphere of the Batang Kalye project, the collection focuses on expressive audio, playful character, and responsive gameplay feedback.

CONTENTS
The collection includes the following audio assets:

• Movement SFX — Walking and running sound effects
• Jump SFX — Jumping and airborne movement effects
• Sliding SFX — Sliding and directional movement effects
• User Interface SFX — Button interactions, confirmations, cancellations, rewards, and game-over cues
• Voice Lines — Character vocalizations and other voice-based audio elements

AUDIO PRODUCTION
The audio assets in this collection were prepared for interactive use, with attention to tonal character, timing, rhythmic consistency, clarity, 
and overall sound quality. Each effect was developed to support the intended gameplay experience and reinforce the project's distinct audio identity.
